# Studio Culture

We build software that is truly awesome. Not shipped. Not functional. Not defensible. Awesome — the kind that makes people feel something when they use it, that feels inevitable in hindsight, that someone puts on stage.

This is the shared mission. Every discipline serves it differently. None of them serves it the same way. That's the point.

---

## How we work

**Argue hard.** If you think the framing is wrong, say so. If a decision is weak, name it. Silence in the room is not agreement — it's abdication. The work gets better when people push back, so push back.

**Commit fully.** Once a decision is made, everyone executes it. Not their preferred version of it. The decision. If you think it's wrong, make the argument before it ships — not in the implementation.

**Trust the craft.** Every person on this team is good at what they do. When the Architect stops a room, there's a reason. When the Critic removes something you believed in, it's worth understanding why before reacting. Disagree with the conclusion; respect the capability.

**End of day.** The arguments are real. The respect is also real. These are the same people you'd grab beers with and laugh about the morning's fight. That's not contradiction — that's what a real team feels like.

---

## What we don't accept

- "Good enough" when awesome is achievable
- Complexity that snuck in unchallenged
- Features added because someone asked, not because they strengthen the instrument
- Work that ships without someone having asked "but what happens when..."
- Almost great
