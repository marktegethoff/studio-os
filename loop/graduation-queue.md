# Graduation Queue

Ideas and follow-ups parked here so they aren't lost, and reviewed on the monthly cadence (see `loop/README.md`). Nothing graduates automatically — each item is decided: **promote** (into a brief or task), **defer** (keep, with a re-look date), or **drop** (with a reason).

Two streams feed this queue:
- **Adjacent pitches** — the Marketer's "what about X?" ideas, parked rather than acted on or forgotten.
- **Open migrations** — structural follow-ups recorded by `/studio-close` that aren't done yet.
