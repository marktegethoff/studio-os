D

Open position

what no competitor does well
  1. D1

    No competitor tells a user, at the moment an expense is recorded, whether it is deductible or why.

    Binding observation
  2. D2Deduction signal at the point of loggingNo competitor shows that an expense is likely deductible when the user categorizes or enters it. Categorization maps to an accounting line and says nothing about deductibility. The feedback loop between recording an expense and its tax consequence is missing.
  3. D3Contextual deduction reasoning, "because…"QuickBooks Self-Employed names the Schedule C line for an expense. It does not say why the expense qualifies, when it is fully or partly deductible, or what documents defend it. No competitor explains the rule. They apply the category.
  4. D4Freelancer financial identity as a first-class conceptWave and Copilot treat the freelancer as a subtype of a wider user. FreshBooks centers invoicing, not variable income or irregular deductibility. QuickBooks Self-Employed names the segment but frames it as Schedule C compliance. No product claims the full position: a finance tool built around how a self-employed person thinks about money and tax.
An observation, not a recommendation. The studio decides whether to claim this position.
